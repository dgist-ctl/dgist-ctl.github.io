About

Published quarterly by the DGIST Center for Teaching & Learning. Each issue covers AI-in-teaching material for higher education published within the previous 12 months, includes only sources whose originals can be verified, and labels every source by type ([Research] > [Preprint] > [Institutional Report] > [Open Resource]). All sources are checked against the originals before publication (bibliographic details, document type, data-collection period, retractions and corrections).

This report is prepared independently by DGIST CTL and receives no external sponsorship.
Views are those of the original authors and may differ from the official position of DGIST CTL. Please cite the original sources.
