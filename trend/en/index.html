Editorial principles

Inclusion

Each issue covers AI-in-teaching material for higher education published within the previous 12 months, and includes only sources whose originals can be verified.

Evidence labels

Every source is labelled by type — [Research] > [Preprint] > [Institutional Report] > [Open Resource]. All sources are checked against the originals before publication (bibliographic details, document type, data-collection period, retractions and corrections).

Independence

Prepared independently by DGIST CTL with no external sponsorship. Views are those of the original authors and may differ from the official position of DGIST CTL. Please cite the original sources.
